# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22

First release.

- Neon Text window: 3D tube text from any TTF/OTF font, live in the scene. One line or outline, round tube or square LED strip, full or open back.
- `Custom/Neon` shader for any mesh: white-hot core, halo (can be switched off entirely), matte glass when off, flicker effects.
- Example signs as a sample.
